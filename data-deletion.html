Data Deletion Instructions

If you want your data removed from this application, send a deletion request through the connected Instagram account and include enough information to identify your conversation.

How to request deletion

  • Open a direct message with the connected Instagram account
  • State that you want your data deleted
  • Provide the Instagram username used in the conversation

What happens next

  • The operator reviews the request
  • Matching conversation and lead records are deleted or anonymized where legally permitted
  • A confirmation is sent back through Instagram when processing is complete

Exceptions

Certain records may be retained where required for legal compliance, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legitimate business obligations.

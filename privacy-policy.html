Privacy Policy

This application helps an Instagram professional account receive, process, and reply to direct messages for lead qualification and customer support.

What data may be processed

  • Instagram public profile identifiers needed to continue a conversation
  • Direct message content sent to the connected Instagram account
  • Lead qualification details voluntarily shared in chat, such as property preferences, budget, or contact details
  • Technical logs required for security, delivery, and troubleshooting

Why data is used

  • to receive and answer Instagram Direct messages
  • to route qualified leads to a manager
  • to maintain conversation history and service reliability
  • to improve response quality and prevent abuse

Data sharing

Data is only used for operating the connected Instagram messaging workflow and may be shared with service providers required for hosting, queueing, AI message generation, and manager notifications.

Retention

Conversation and lead data is retained only as long as needed for sales communication, customer support, analytics, legal compliance, or deletion requests.

User rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data by contacting the app operator through the connected Instagram account or the contact details listed in the app profile.
